# AniView 2.7.0

Release candidate: `2.7.0+38`, stable channel.

## Release notes

- **Watch parties:** start a party, share the invite link or QR code, and everyone plays, pauses and seeks together, with chat, reactions and per-person mute. A phone can bring the TV into the party. Parties use the AniView relay (`wss://aniview.ebrahim.co.tz`) first, with public Nostr relays as fallback.
- **Torrents:** releases from Nyaa, with SeaDex's best and alternative picks ranked first and more title variants searched. Stream or download through TorBox or Real-Debrid, or peer to peer on desktop through the bundled rqbit. AniView remembers the release group you last played for each show.
- **Anime4K upscaling** in the player on desktop and Android, with presets.
- **Subtitle timing:** shift subtitles earlier or later while an episode plays (Subtitles → Adjust timing; Z and X on desktop).
- **Downloads multiselect:** long-press to pick several downloads, then delete them or retry the failed ones.
- **Settings, regrouped:** settings opens on a list of groups, each on its own page.
- **Dub subtitles:** choose which subtitles a dub starts with.
- **Performance:** home, search, extensions, the player and download progress rebuild far less; posters are kept on disk between launches.
- **Sources:** every source was checked live and plays and downloads again (ani.pm, KickAssAnime, AnimeStream, Anikoto, animepahe). Anikoto is back. Sites behind Cloudflare are fetched through the browser that holds the clearance.
- **Fixes:** TV navigation and focus, home sections can be reordered with a remote, Rewatch when every episode is watched, deleting a download frees space on a full disk, failing servers fall through to the next, the player recovers from errors mid-episode, and theme colours are consistent in dark mode.

## Removed

- The US$1/month subscription survey, its Settings → About entry and its stored answer key. No subscription is introduced.
