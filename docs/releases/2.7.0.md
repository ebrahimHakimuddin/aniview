# AniView 2.7.0

Release candidate: `2.7.0+38`, stable channel.

## Release notes

- **Watch parties:** start a party, share the invite link or QR code, and everyone plays, pauses and seeks together, with chat, reactions and per-person mute. A phone can bring the TV into the party. Parties use the AniView relay (`wss://aniview.ebrahim.co.tz`) first, with public Nostr relays as fallback.
- **Torrents:** releases from Nyaa, with SeaDex's best and alternative picks ranked first and more title variants searched. Stream or download through TorBox or Real-Debrid, or peer to peer on desktop through the bundled rqbit. AniView remembers the release group you last played for each show.
  > AniView doesn't host, upload or distribute any content, and isn't liable for what you stream or download through torrents, debrid services or peers. You're responsible for using them in line with the laws where you live.
- **Anime4K upscaling** in the player on desktop and Android, with presets.
- **Feedback:** send a bug report or feature request straight from Settings → About → Feedback.
- **Sources:** every source was checked live and plays and downloads again (ani.pm, KickAssAnime, AnimeStream, Anikoto, animepahe). Anikoto is back. Sites behind Cloudflare are fetched through the browser that holds the clearance.
- **UI/UX enhancements:**
  - Fix subtitles that are out of sync from the player's Subtitles menu → Adjust timing (Z and X on desktop).
  - Long-press a download to select several, then delete them or retry the failed ones together.
  - Settings opens on a list of groups, each on its own page.
  - Choose which subtitles a dub starts with.
  - TV: focus always shows on remote-only devices, titles and actions sit inside the safe area, home sections can be reordered with a remote, and the seek hint names its keys.
  - Rewatch is offered when every episode is watched, and the skip and play buttons follow the theme's accent colour.
- **Performance:** home, search, extensions, the player and download progress redraw far less, and posters stay cached between launches.
- **Fixes:** progress saved while watching shows on Home straight away, leaving a party reaches everyone on public relays, the Linux AppImage uses its own bundled libraries, deleting a download frees space on a full disk, a failing server falls through to the next, and the player recovers from errors mid-episode.

## Removed

- The US$1/month subscription survey, its Settings → About entry and its stored answer key. No subscription is introduced.
